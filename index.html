How Vaitbrige Traced $1.2M in Stolen Crypto Across 5 Exchanges

When a UK client came to Vaitbrige after losing $1.2M to a fraudulent crypto exchange, the trail looked cold. The funds had already been moved through several wallets, split into smaller amounts, and passed through multiple platforms designed to obscure their origin. Our recovery team used on-chain analysis to follow the transaction graph across five separate exchanges, correlating wallet clusters with know-your-customer records where available and flagging the off-ramps most likely to be used by the fraudsters. Working alongside the client's bank and two international exchanges, Vaitbrige built a case file strong enough to trigger a freeze on the remaining funds while legal channels were pursued. Within four months, the client received a full recovery. Crypto fraud is one of the fastest growing categories of case we see at Vaitbrige, and every case is different. If you believe you have lost funds to a fraudulent exchange or wallet, the earlier you contact us, the more of the trail is still intact.

The Anatomy of a Romance Scam and How Vaitbrige Fights Back

Romance scams rarely start with a request for money. They start with weeks, sometimes months, of carefully built trust — matching interests, consistent messaging, and a story engineered to explain why the two of you can never quite meet in person. Once trust is established, the requests begin small: help with a phone bill, a plane ticket to finally visit. They escalate quickly once the victim has already committed emotionally, often coinciding with a fabricated crisis such as a medical emergency or a business opportunity. Vaitbrige's recovery process starts with a full timeline of communications and payments, which our team uses to identify the accounts and payment rails involved. From there we work with banks, payment processors and, where relevant, international partners to pursue a return of funds. If something about an online relationship does not add up, trust that instinct. Vaitbrige's team has seen these patterns before and can help you understand what happened and what can still be done.

Stay alert

New UK Financial Regulations: What They Mean for Scam Victims

The 2025 regulatory updates strengthened the obligations UK banks have when a customer reports an authorised push payment (APP) scam, extending reimbursement protections and tightening the timeline banks must respond within. For Vaitbrige clients, this means stronger leverage when we approach a bank on a client's behalf: institutions now carry clearer liability for failing to intervene on suspicious transfers, and reimbursement claims are easier to substantiate with a well documented case file. The changes also increased information-sharing requirements between banks, which has already sped up several of our active cases by making it easier to trace funds once they leave the victim's account. Regulation moves slowly, but every update like this shifts the balance further in favour of victims. Vaitbrige's legal team tracks these changes closely so every case we take on makes full use of the protections available.

Recovery scammers may claim to be regulators, lawyers, exchanges, or specialist investigators. Warning signs include guarantees, urgent demands, unexplained upfront fees, or requests for banking credentials and remote access.
