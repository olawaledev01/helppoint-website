Ask us a question →
Can you guarantee my money will be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on the facts, payment method, timing, and whether funds can be traced or returned. Anyone promising a guaranteed result should be treated with caution.

Should I pay an upfront fee?

Don’t rush. Ask for a written agreement that explains every fee, what work will be done, possible extra costs, and what happens if recovery is unsuccessful.

What should I do first?

Contact the bank or platform you used through its official website or the number on your card. Ask whether the payment can be stopped or recalled, and report suspected account access immediately.

What information should I never share?

Never share passwords, one-time passcodes, private keys, or wallet recovery phrases. No legitimate recovery conversation needs your seed phrase.

Is the form secure and active?

This concept site is a front-end demo. The form does not send or store information. A real business should connect it to an appropriately secured service and publish a privacy notice before collecting personal details.
